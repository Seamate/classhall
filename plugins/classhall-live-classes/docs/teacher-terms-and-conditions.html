Classhall Live-Class Teacher Terms and Conditions

These terms apply to teachers who apply to create and teach live classes on Classhall. By submitting a live-class teacher application, you confirm that you have read, understood, and accepted these terms.

1. Teacher Eligibility

You must provide accurate information about your identity, qualifications, teaching experience, subjects taught, classes taught, and payment details. Classhall may approve, reject, suspend, verify, or unverify a teacher profile at its discretion.

2. Accuracy of Information

You agree that the information submitted in your application and profile is true and current. You must update Classhall if your teaching information, contact information, or bank details change.

3. Teacher Approval and Verification

Approval allows you to create live classes on Classhall. Verification is a separate trust signal and may be granted, removed, or withheld by Classhall. Approval does not automatically guarantee permanent verification.

4. Class Creation

You must create live classes only for subjects and class levels you can competently teach. Class titles, schedules, descriptions, prices, class types, and meeting details must be accurate and not misleading.

5. Class Schedule

You are responsible for selecting a realistic schedule and being available at the scheduled time. For recurring classes, you are responsible for all sessions included in the class package.

6. Meeting Links and Access

You must provide a valid private meeting link for each live class. Meeting links must not be shared publicly. You should admit only students who booked through Classhall.

7. Professional Conduct

You must teach in a respectful, safe, and professional manner. You must not harass, abuse, exploit, discriminate against, or behave inappropriately toward students, parents, guardians, or Classhall staff.

8. Student Privacy

You must protect student information. You may not misuse student names, emails, phone numbers, meeting details, screenshots, recordings, or any other information obtained through Classhall.

9. Payments Outside Classhall

You must not ask students or parents to pay outside Classhall for classes booked or discovered through Classhall unless Classhall has given written permission.

10. Earnings and Payouts

Teacher earnings are recorded after paid bookings. Classhall may deduct its commission or platform fee before payout. Payouts are processed manually to the bank details supplied by the teacher, subject to Classhall's payout process and fraud checks.

11. Cancellations, Missed Classes, and Disputes

If you cannot hold a scheduled class, you must notify Classhall and affected students as early as possible. Classhall may review missed classes, student complaints, attendance, booking records, and payment records when resolving disputes.

12. Reviews and Ratings

Students may rate and review teachers after classes. Classhall may moderate reviews before public display. You must not pressure students to leave false reviews or retaliate against students for honest reviews.

13. Prohibited Content and Behaviour

You must not use live classes to promote harmful content, illegal activity, hate, abuse, academic fraud, impersonation, spam, or unrelated commercial services. Classhall may remove classes or suspend accounts that violate this rule.

14. Suspension or Removal

Classhall may suspend or remove a teacher, class, payout eligibility, or public profile if there is suspected fraud, policy violation, student safety concern, repeated missed classes, misleading information, or any other serious issue.

15. Platform Changes

Classhall may update live-class features, teacher requirements, payout rules, review rules, and these terms from time to time. Continued use of the live-class teacher feature after updates means you accept the updated terms.

16. Agreement

By submitting the teacher application form, you agree to follow these terms and any additional Classhall policies that apply to teachers, users, payments, privacy, and acceptable use.
