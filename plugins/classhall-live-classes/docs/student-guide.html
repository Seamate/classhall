Classhall Live Classes: Student and Parent Guide

Classhall Live Classes helps students and parents book online classes with teachers who can explain specific Classhall subjects in real time.

What Students Can Do

  • Find teachers by subject, class, or teacher name
  • View teacher profiles, ratings, subjects, and reviews
  • Book one-on-one or group live classes
  • Pay securely through Classhall checkout
  • Join booked classes from the student dashboard
  • Confirm that a class held
  • Rate and review teachers after completed classes

Finding a Teacher

Use the Find a Teacher page to search for live-class teachers. You can search by teacher name, subject, or class. You can also filter by class level, such as JSS1 or SS1.

Teacher cards show important information such as:

  • Teacher name
  • Verification status
  • Rating
  • Number of reviews
  • Completed classes
  • Subjects taught
  • Classes taught

Finding Live Classes for a Subject

On supported Classhall subject pages, a live-class section may appear automatically. This section shows available live classes for that subject and can link to more live-class options.

Booking a Class

  1. Open the live class or subject page.
  2. Review the teacher, schedule, class type, price, and available seats.
  3. Click the booking button.
  4. Complete payment through checkout.
  5. Open your live classes dashboard to see the booking.

One-Time and Recurring Classes

A live class may be a one-time class or a recurring class. Recurring classes can run weekly or on multiple days per week for one month. When you book a recurring class, you are booking the full class package shown on the page.

Schedule Conflicts

Classhall prevents students from booking two live classes that happen at the same time. If a class conflicts with another booked class, you will need to book a different class, possibly from another teacher.

Joining a Class

After payment is confirmed, open your live classes dashboard. The join button and meeting link are shown only during the allowed access window around the class time.

This protects the meeting link and helps ensure that only booked students can join.

Email Notifications

Classhall sends booking confirmation emails after successful payment. When reminders are enabled, Classhall also sends reminder emails before class sessions.

After a Class

After the class has ended, you can confirm that the class held. Once the booking is ready for review, you can rate the teacher and write a review.

Reviews

Your review helps other students and parents choose teachers. Reviews may be checked by Classhall before they appear publicly.

You can update a review for a class you already reviewed. Updating a review does not create an extra review count for the teacher.

Safety and Good Use

  • Join classes only through your Classhall dashboard.
  • Do not share meeting links with other users.
  • Be respectful to teachers and other students.
  • Use the review feature honestly after attending a class.
  • Contact Classhall support if there is a problem with a class or booking.
