Classhall Live Classes: Teacher Guide

Classhall Live Classes lets qualified teachers earn extra income by teaching Classhall students online. Teachers can apply, create live classes for subjects they teach, receive bookings, hold classes through meeting platforms such as Google Meet or Zoom, and track earnings.

How to Become a Live-Class Teacher

  1. Log in to your Classhall account.
  2. Open the teacher application page.
  3. Complete your profile, teaching details, and payment details.
  4. Accept the live-class teacher terms and conditions.
  5. Submit the application for Classhall admin review.

Admin approval is required before a teacher can create live classes. Verification is managed separately by Classhall and may be shown on your public teacher profile.

Teacher Application Information

The application form asks for:

  • Display name
  • Professional biography
  • Qualifications
  • Teaching experience
  • Subjects taught
  • Classes taught
  • Exam specialisations
  • Bank name, account name, and account number for payouts

Provide accurate information. Classhall may reject, suspend, or unverify profiles that contain misleading information.

Creating a Live Class

Once approved, open your teacher dashboard and create a live class.

You will choose:

  • The class level, such as JSS1 or SS1
  • The subject
  • Class type: one-on-one or group
  • Delivery platform, such as Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or another platform
  • Private meeting URL
  • Date, time, and duration
  • Schedule type: one time, weekly, or multiple days per week
  • Price
  • Seats available, if the class is a group class

Meeting Links

Create your meeting link on the selected platform, copy the link, and paste it into the private meeting URL field. The meeting link is not shown publicly. Booked students can access it only from their dashboard during the configured join window.

One-Time and Recurring Classes

A one-time class has one session. A weekly class runs once per week for one month. A multiple-days-per-week class runs on selected days for one month.

Recurring classes are booked as a single class package. Do not create separate duplicate classes for every recurring session unless each one is meant to be sold separately.

Class Approval

Depending on Classhall settings, a newly created class may require admin approval before students can book it. Classes that are rejected or suspended will not be available for booking.

Before the Class

  • Make sure the meeting link is correct.
  • Be available before the scheduled start time.
  • Admit only booked students.
  • Do not share private meeting links outside Classhall.
  • Prepare the topic so the class is focused and useful.

Reminder Emails

When reminders are enabled, Classhall sends reminder emails to teachers and booked students before upcoming sessions. The reminder email includes class details and dashboard links.

After the Class

Students can confirm that the class held and leave a review. Reviews may require admin approval before they appear publicly.

Ratings and Reviews

Your public rating is based on approved student reviews. Students can update a previous review for the same class booking, but it is not counted as a new review.

To improve your rating:

  • Start on time.
  • Explain clearly.
  • Use examples that match the student's class level.
  • Keep communication respectful and professional.
  • End with a quick recap and next steps.

Earnings and Payouts

Classhall records teacher earnings from paid bookings. Your teacher dashboard shows earnings and payout history. Payouts are processed manually by Classhall using the bank details you submitted.

Teacher Responsibilities

  • Teach only subjects you are qualified to teach.
  • Keep your profile and bank details accurate.
  • Do not request students to pay outside Classhall for booked classes.
  • Do not misuse student contact details.
  • Do not share meeting links with unbooked users.
  • Report issues to Classhall as soon as possible.
